About the 3D Model

This educational visualization shows the design and components of an RBMK nuclear power plant, the type of reactor used at Chernobyl. Explore the structure to better understand the technology behind the site and its history.

This virtual experience is for educational purposes. The 3D exhibit is hosted by Energy Encyclopedia and requires an internet connection.
